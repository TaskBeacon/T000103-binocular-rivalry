# Task Plot Brief

## Task

- Title: Binocular Rivalry
- Construct: bistable visual awareness / spontaneous perceptual alternation
- Paradigm: continuous report of the currently dominant percept while incompatible red and cyan gratings are presented dichoptically through anaglyph glasses.

## Rows

- Red-left / cyan-right: red grating tilts left; cyan grating tilts right.
- Red-right / cyan-left: red grating tilts right; cyan grating tilts left.

## Trial Timeline

1. Alignment: 2 s. View the dichoptic grating inside the fusion frame; no response.
2. Rivalry report: 120 s. Press F when the left-tilted grating dominates, J when the right-tilted grating dominates, and Space when the percept is mixed. Report every perceptual change.
3. ITI: 2 s. Fixation only; no response.

## Notes

- The physical stimulus remains constant during the 120 s report window; the reported percept changes spontaneously.
- The two rows counterbalance color-to-orientation and eye assignment while preserving the response mapping.
- The stimulus is a circular aperture containing superimposed red and cyan diagonal gratings, centered in a black-and-white fusion frame with a central fixation marker.
